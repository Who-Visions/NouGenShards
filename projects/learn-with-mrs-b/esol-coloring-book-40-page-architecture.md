# Learn With Mrs. B: My First English Coloring & Activity Book

**Production grid, v2 (2026-09-14).** 40 interior pages plus front and back matter. Built to the full build brief (relay leg 20260914T132643Z), which supersedes v1.

## Brand frame
- **Learn With Mrs. B** (learnwithmrsb.com) is K-12 tutoring in Palm Beach County by Mrs. B. She has 25+ years of teaching, is an FAU graduate (Class of 2001), and holds a master's specializing in ESOL. Services: Reading, Math, French, ESOL.
- **Voice:** warm, confidence-first, academically credible, never remedial. The book should feel like Mrs. B's tutoring expanded into publishing, not a worksheet pack.
- **Series:** this is title #1 of a branded series. Unit structure, icon set and Mrs. B character are reusable.

## System
- **Audience:** K-1 English learners, WIDA levels 1-3, multilingual families.
- **Core loop, one step per page in every unit:** **SEE** (meet the words) > **SAY** (use the frame aloud) > **COLOR** (comprehend and color) > **TRACE** (letter, word, frame) > **USE** (produce independently).
- **Gradual release inside each unit:** full model > trace/copy > fill the blank > choose/match > independent word or sentence.
- **Language load:** 5-8 target words and 1-2 sentence frames per unit, repeated on all 5 pages and recycled in later units.
- **Icons**, with 3-5 word directions: 👀 SEE, 🗣 SAY, 🖍 COLOR, ✏️ TRACE, ✍️ WRITE, 🔗 MATCH.
- **Visual rules:** thick outlines (3-4 pt at print size), big open coloring fields, one learning goal per page, uncluttered scenes, authentic multicultural children and families, generous writing lines (K-1 ruled, dashed midline), Mrs. B as a recurring guide.
- **Page template:** header with unit color tab and step icon; a one-line direction; the art; a writing/answer zone; a footer strip with the unit's word bank and a small Mrs. B tip for the grown-up.

Fields for every page: **Objective**, **ESOL target**, **Vocabulary**, **Frame**, **Activity**, **Illustration brief**, **Teacher/parent prompt**, **Review connection**.

## Unit 1: Me & Feelings (pp. 1-5)
Words: hello, name, happy, sad, mad, scared, tired. Frames: **My name is ___.** / **I feel ___.** Letter: **Hh**.

**p1 · SEE: Hello, I'm Me!**
- Objective: greet and say own name. ESOL target: greeting (WIDA: identify self).
- Vocabulary: hello, name. Frame: "Hello! My name is ___."
- Activity: color Mrs. B waving; draw self in the empty frame beside her.
- Illustration brief: Mrs. B at a classroom door, waving; open picture frame; speech bubble with the frame.
- Teacher/parent prompt: "Say hello to Mrs. B. Tell her your name."
- Review connection: name returns on p40 (certificate) and p39.

**p2 · SAY: How Do I Feel?**
- Objective: name 5 feelings. ESOL target: label emotions.
- Vocabulary: happy, sad, mad, scared, tired. Frame: "I feel ___."
- Activity: point to each face and say the frame; match faces to words.
- Illustration brief: 5 large child faces, clearly different expressions, with word labels.
- Teacher/parent prompt: "Make the same face. Say: I feel happy."
- Review connection: feelings reused in p10, p30, p38.

**p3 · COLOR: Feelings Park**
- Objective: understand feeling words in a scene. ESOL target: listen and identify.
- Vocabulary: unit words. Frame: "She feels ___." (receptive)
- Activity: "Color the happy child yellow. Color the sad child blue."
- Illustration brief: park with 4 children showing different feelings; wide open shapes.
- Teacher/parent prompt: read each color direction aloud; the child points first, then colors.
- Review connection: colors preview Unit 4.

**p4 · TRACE: H is for Hello**
- Objective: letter-sound /h/; write "hi". ESOL target: sound-letter link.
- Vocabulary: hello, happy, hi. Frame: trace "I feel happy."
- Activity: trace Hh on a hopscotch path; trace the frame.
- Illustration brief: hopscotch with dotted Hh letters; happy sun.
- Teacher/parent prompt: "Hh says /h/. Hello, happy. What else starts with /h/?"
- Review connection: letter strip collects on p36.

**p5 · USE: My Face Today**
- Objective: express own feeling in writing. ESOL target: independent frame completion.
- Vocabulary: unit words. Frame: "My name is ___. I feel ___."
- Activity: draw your face today; complete both frames with the word bank.
- Illustration brief: large mirror outline; word bank tiles; Mrs. B "You did it!" badge.
- Teacher/parent prompt: "Read your sentences to me."
- Review connection: same frames in the p39 "All About Me" page.
