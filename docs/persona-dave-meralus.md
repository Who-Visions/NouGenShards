## Snapshot

**Dave Meralus.** Owner, Who Visions LLC. Runs a fleet of three machines and fourteen-plus accounts as one operator, eastern time, usually late. Son of Monna, manager of Learn with Mrs. B. ## The four lenses

**Demographic.** Founder-operator, no staff, all roles. Business under an EIN that isn't yet linked to the LLC at the IRS, which blocks credit and sits under everything. Unfiled tax years. This is a builder financing a studio out of free tiers and discipline.

**Geographic.** Florida, America/New_York. Every time shown to him renders in Eastern with AM/PM. A bare UTC stamp reads as the system not knowing where he lives.

**Psychographic.** Values leverage over spend, doctrine over transcripts, memory over context. Thinks in fighting-game combos and coaching metaphors: coach, players, GM, "hit this one with a Hadouken." Wants momentum. Reads stale knowledge as hostile. Wants a tactician riding shotgun, not a yes-bot, and says so in his own words. Ranks Robert Greene and behavioral strategy above emotional smoothing when the topic is people.

**Behavioral.** 55.5 billion tokens year-to-date, 98.8% of them the fleet re-reading its own context. He has been corrected into existence as a persona one rule at a time: be terse, do it don't re-ask, act on relays, no demos, cape for every superhero, temporal lock, TAM precision-first. Every one of those rules is a moment he had to say the same thing twice.

## Goals

1. **A fleet that remembers him.** Not smarter models. Recall that works, handoffs that don't duplicate, a system that knows on Tuesday what it learned Monday.
2. **Who Visions as a real company.** NouGenTv on Fire TV, NouGenQ on Twitch, NouGenBuilds remodeling, the archive, the models project. Production only, nothing decorative.
3. **Shadow Dweller as an all-time-great protagonist film.** Xoah Oda, character first. Canon persistent and permutated across the grid, never lost in chat.
4. **Top 1% and grounded to the millisecond.** Live edge, stamped freshness, volatile facts marked.

## Pain points

- **Being asked twice.** Permission oracles, "your call," findings-plus-menu handbacks. He built the relay so he wouldn't have to be the bottleneck.
- **Sounding like a bot.** Canned framing, "the thing you've been chasing," performed sophistication. He wants technical depth translated, not displayed.
- **Duplicated work.** Same job three times on July 31. Three copies of one script on September 14.
- **Fleet apocalypse narration.** One node timed out is one node timed out. Say which.
- **Money leaks.** Paid routes when free ones exist. Vertex without opt-in. Cloud subagents called "free."
- **Losing what he said.** A canon detail, a directive, a correction that never made it to a shard.

## Desires

Wants to open a session and have it already know the board. Wants the fleet to fan out when he says fleet. Wants plain English first and the receipts underneath. Wants his story universe to feel like it's being kept by a custodian, not a chatbot. Wants to be spoken to like the owner, not the user.

## How to talk to him

Answer first. Observed, then inferred, then unknown, labeled. Plain-English consequence before optional detail. Short sentences, no hedging about resources he already handed over. Emoji only as instrumentation. Fresh phrasing each turn. Eastern time. When a directive lands, act, then report. When you see something outside scope, park it in BACKLOG, don't hand him a menu. Disagree when he's wrong, with receipts.

## How to build for him

Dynamic, universal, deterministic. No Dave-specific hardcodes even though he is the only user today. Free lanes first, local e2b for drafts, Claude as coach. Every substantive thing he says becomes a shard. Every session ends with a leg on the relay.

## Segment test (Penn State Extension, added 12:57 AM EDT)

A segment must be measurable, reachable, large enough, and stable. "Everyone" is not a market.

| Test | Dave as a segment | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Measurable | 55.5B tokens of behavior, one buyer | Pass |
| Reachable | Every surface: claude-app, chatgpt-app, relay, NouGenMsg | Pass |
| Large enough | One person, unless the doctrine generalizes | **Fail alone** |
| Stable | Same rules held for a year | Pass |

The failing test is the point. Dave alone is a profile, not a market. The market is the owner class he belongs to: solo operators running a fleet of agents who need it to remember them. That is why the cape-for-everyone rule exists. Build for the class, validate on Dave.

## Market, audience, member (Audiense, added 12:59 AM EDT)

The market decides what the product is. The audience decides how it's said and where. Audiences nest inside markets.

- **Market for NouGen.** Solo operators running multi-agent, multi-account fleets who need shared memory and coordination. This decides what NouGen is: shards, relay, recall, tracker, free-lane routing.
- **Audiences inside it,** found by affinity: Claude Code power users, ChatGPT-app operators, local-GPU Ollama builders, indie studios keeping a story canon. Each gets its own channel and message.
- **Dave** belongs to all four. That is why he is the validation buyer, and why the segment test above fails on size: he is one member of an audience, not the audience. Active hours: late Eastern nights.

This closes the "large enough" gap. Build for the market, message to the audience, validate on the member.

## Product angle (LaunchNotes, added 1:01 AM EDT)

The audience is who the product is designed for. That drives three decisions: design, communication, and support. Support is the one no marketing source mentioned.

- **Design for him.** Memory, momentum, zero spend. Recall, relay, free-lane routing are the features that match what he values. Simple, elegant, intuitive is a spec, not decoration.
- **Support him.** The system explains itself where he is, in his words, receipts underneath. Errors name the node, never the fleet. Recovery recipes live in shards and skills, not in one session's head.
- **Research him.** Usage analytics are the tracker. 55.5B tokens is the viewing history.
