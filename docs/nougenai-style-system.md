# NouGenAi Style System Specification

> **Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Classification:** Canonical Visual Architecture & Design Language  
> **Substrate Authority:** `.nougen` & Fleet Design Pipeline

---

## 👁️ Core Philosophy & Definition

**NouGenAi** is a clean, cinematic, cyber-anime visual style built on strong black silhouettes, grounded athletic anatomy, expressive Black and street-animation influence, tactical cyberpunk detail, dense functional environments, and controlled visual effects.

> **Execution Directive:**
> Clean linework. Smooth readable silhouettes. Natural athletic proportions. Soft-to-firm anime facial structure. Precise cybernetic and mechanical detail. Cinematic cyberpunk settings. Restrained palettes. Contextual effects only. Character references must match **1:1** across face, body, outfit, hairstyle, proportions, markings, colors, and silhouette.

### 🚫 Non-Negotiable Hard Negative Rules
* ❌ No dry-brush edges, scratchy ink, jagged strokes, chaotic splatter, rough brush texture.
* ❌ No random glitch, constant aura, default spiky silhouettes, stretched limbs, distorted anatomy.
* ❌ No over-sharp faces, aggressive manga inking, messy speed marks, or unmotivated visual noise.

---

## 1. Linework Architecture
* **Controlled & Animation-Ready:** Clean contour flow with expressive, smooth pressure shifts.
* **Fabric Tension:** Readable folds, seams, and fabric weight across streetwear and tactical gear.
* **Technical Hard Surfaces:** Thin, exact, engineering-grade lines for machines, armor, weapons, HUD interfaces, and architecture.
* **Density Hierarchy:**
  * **Faces:** Low density (smooth, uncluttered, expressive).
  * **Hair, Apparel, Tactical Rig:** Medium density.
  * **Cybernetics, Hydraulics, Architecture:** High technical density.
  * **Impact Zones:** High impact clarity; zero messy clutter.

## 2. Shape Language & Anatomy
* **Silhouettes:** Grounded athletic builds, strong core posture, balanced human proportions, and intentional tactical asymmetry (straps, modular plates, asymmetric utility pouches).
* **Facial Geometry:** Human, softer geometry with distinct brows, cheeks, and mouth shapes. Avoid perpetual razor jaws, permanent predator expressions, or over-rendered hardness.
* **Anatomy & Weight:** Grounded center-of-gravity, athletic torsion, martial balance, and physical weight. No unnatural limb elongation or caricature deformation.

## 3. Eyes & Psychological States
Eyes anchor psychological intent without defaulting to extreme caricatures:
* **Calm / Observational:** Clean contours, minimal highlights, relaxed eyelid drape.
* **Combat Focus:** Heightened contrast, narrowed pupils, sharp brow tension.
* **Awakening / Ego Surge:** Intricate iris micro-detail, selective sharp glow, tight cinematic framing.
* **Network / Direct Neural Link:** Subtle HUD digital reflections, scan-line artifacts, micro-data runes.
* **Stress / Shock:** Constricted pupils, deeper ocular orbital shadows, controlled perspective tension.
*(Rule: Never default to permanent fluorescent iris glow or multi-fractured anime reflections).*

## 4. Hair Architecture
* **Graphic Readability:** Instant silhouette recognition via clean graphic masses.
* **Mass Separation:** Smooth chunk division; hair flows strictly according to physical motion and force vectors. No jagged brush strokes or arbitrary slash highlights.

## 5. Color Spectrum & Ratios
NouGenAi employs restrained, cinematic grading:

* **Base Foundation:** Deep black, carbon charcoal, cool blue-gray, concrete beige, military olive green, rust brown, bone white, muted skin tones, and desaturated urban asphalt.
* **Tactical Accents (Used Sparingly $\le 5\%$):** Blood red, cyber cyan, neon lime, warning hazard orange, electric blue, gold/amber neural glow.
* **Readability Guarantee:** Character values must cleanly detach from background values. Aura and glow only activate when narrative or cybernetic logic dictates.

## 6. Shadow, Lighting & Value Distribution
* **Standard Frame Distribution:**
  * **50%** Dark mass / deep black
  * **30%** Midtone
  * **15%** Key light / highlight
  * **5%** Selective glow / accent
* **High-Contrast Tactical Frame:**
  * **60%** Dark mass
  * **25%** Midtone
  * **10%** Light
  * **5%** Accent
* **Shading Profile:** Clean cel-shaded vector cuts, smooth ambient occlusion, strong rim highlights. No gritty brush shading or accidental splatter.

## 7. Functional Cyberpunk Environments
* **Urban & Industrial Spaces:** Concrete, conduits, fiber bundles, holographic municipal signage, rain slick, CCTV clusters, brutalist apartments, transit corridors, and high-tension suburban fringes.
* **Usable Architecture:** Foreground depth layering, accessible panels, operational vents, and realistic scale. Backgrounds support the narrative subject without shouting over character silhouette.
* **Atmospheric Volumetrics:** Rain streaks, localized steam/smoke, lens flare, reflection pools, evening atmospheric haze, fluorescent tube hum.

## 8. Composition & Framing Dynamics
* **Dominant Focal Anchor:** Every frame features one unmistakable focal point dictated by silhouette.
* **Negative Space:** Cultivates tension, isolation, and philosophical weight.
* **Framing Toolkit:** Extreme tactical close-ups, low-angle dominance cuts, deadpan wide surveillance views, offset Dutch angles for crisis, over-the-shoulder tactical sights, and dynamic diagonal action planes.

## 9. Motion Dynamics & Contact Physics
* **Physical Choreography:** Martial arts weight distribution, sports-impact planting, torso rotation, clear weapon clearance spacing, and crisp contact beats.
* **Motion Artifacts:** Controlled directional blur, crisp shadow echoes, debris dispersion, fabric whip contours, HUD motion trails, and crisp impact flash frames.

## 10. Character Archetypes & Tactical Streetwear
* **Wardrobe Lexicon:** Heavy duster trench coats, compression bodysuits, layered streetwear, combat boots, utility rigging, low-profile ballistic plates, high collar shrouds, oversized hoodies, and augmented ports.
* **Core Archetypes:**
  * Revolutionary child-philosopher
  * Cybernetic tactical operator
  * Ego athlete-warrior
  * Street samurai
  * Corrupt institutional clown-monster
  * Network ghost / phantom runner
  * Corporate/military handler
  * Deceptively innocent machine masking existential dread

## 11. Integrated Cybernetics & Interface Design
* **Mechanical Integration:** Joint seams, modular forearm swap-outs, synthetic muscle striations, cervical spine interfaces, optical HUD reflections, and mechanical fingers.
* **HUD & UI:** Monochromatic or clean dual-tone vector lines, coordinate grids, targeting reticles, biometric bounding boxes, telemetry readouts, and clean sans-serif typography.

## 12. Emotional Acting & Texture Palette
* **Facial Acting:** Controlled baseline emotional containment; stress manifests in ocular tension, jaw clench, and controlled perspective stretch.
* **Permitted Textures:** Fine halftone screen, video noise, rain streaks, concrete grain, brushed steel micro-scratches, digital scan lines, weave patterns, blood mist, and particulate dust.
