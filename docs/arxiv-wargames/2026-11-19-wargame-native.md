### 2611.23621 — Agent Verifiers in Counterfactual Analysis
**Claim:** Agents can expand the covariate space of randomized experiments by retrieving fast-moving local context, but verification is hard: treatment effects are unobserved, retrieval can leak post-treatment information, and encoder weights can leak post-treatment knowledge. The paper proposes a three-layer protocol — pre-treatment date filtering, heterogeneity validation by rank-weighted average treatment effects (RATEs), and encoder admissibility via vintaged encoders, future-fact probes, and weight perturbations — tested on 11 datasets anchored by Uganda's Youth Opportunities Program.
**Native relevance:** P6 (retrieval eval harness) and P2 (embeddings). Date filtering plus provenance checks map onto `retrieve()` (`core.py:4263`): shards carry timestamps and `source_uri`, so a pre-cutoff filter is implementable in SQLite. Encoder admissibility speaks to the onnx MiniLM vs ollama nomic provider/dimension risk in the gap report.
**Verdict:** ADAPT — date filtering and provenance checks fit Native as-is, but RATE validation and encoder vintaging are experiment-specific machinery with no memory-engine counterpart; they need rework into retrieval-quality checks.
**Does NOT prove:** An abstract is not a benchmark. The 6/11 and 7/11 RATE wins and the "no clear evidence of weight leakage" finding cannot be verified from the abstract alone — no effect sizes or per-dataset results are given.

**Verdict:** REJECT — it compresses inference-time KV cache, not stored memory; it conflicts with nothing but serves nothing in a SQLite memory engine.
**Does NOT prove:** The accuracy, memory and speedup figures are abstract-only claims on four open-weight models and eight unnamed benchmarks; the 8.44% lead over the strongest baseline at 256 tokens cannot be verified without the paper's tables, and nothing shows the method transfers off Qwen3-VL-style agents.

On CO2-storage problems in the GEOS simulator, grounding raises structurally valid inputs from 5/20 to 20/20, but acceptance reaches 20/20 only with complete rule-based input preparation; local repairs correct three seeded defects while preserving valid inputs.
**Native relevance:** The architecture pattern, not the domain, is the prize — and it maps onto the real build, P3+P4+P5. Native's replication journal, signed sync and conflict handling do not exist yet, and this paper's rule is the design constraint for them: deterministic routines own construction and verification, the agent only proposes. A sync payload that fails a deterministic signature, sequence or schema check is rejected, never repaired by LLM judgment. The same split applies to P5 restore tests and P6 evaluation: supporting tests that catch simulations passing physics checks while differing from the problem description are the analogue of recall that scores well while returning the wrong shard.
**Verdict:** ADAPT — the deterministic-checks-own-the-system pattern fits Native's sync and recovery work exactly, but the GEOS/CO2 domain is irrelevant and the rules must be written fresh for shard journals, node identities and conflicts.
**Does NOT prove:** The evidence is 80 attempts on two flow problems; validity rates that small do not establish general reliability, and nothing in the abstract shows the pattern transfers from physics simulation to memory synchronization.

### 2605.19049 — KVBuffer: IO-aware Serving for Linear Attention
**Claim:** KVBuffer is an IO-aware serving mechanism for linear attention that buffers recent keys and values so decoding can use chunkwise computation (deferring state updates and applying them in batch), verify speculative draft tokens in parallel without temporary states, and serve short contexts directly from the buffer without creating attention state. Implemented in SGLang for Qwen3.5 and Nemotron-3, it speeds batch decoding up to 1.47x and increases max serving requests 5x for speculative decoding with four draft tokens.
**Native relevance:** None direct. Native performs no attention computation and no token decoding; its vector lane is a per-DB matrix cache (`core.py:3684` `_vector_retrieve`) plus the unwired memmapped `ann_index.py`, neither of which is a linear-attention state. The only portable idea is batching deferred updates — the same shape as batching embedding-index rebuilds rather than updating per write — which is a P6 index-freshness concern, but the paper's mechanism (KV buffering around a recurrent attention state) has no counterpart in the engine.
**Verdict:** REJECT — a serving-layer optimization for linear-attention LLMs, not a retrieval or storage mechanism; Native has no decoding loop to accelerate.
**Does NOT prove:** The 1.47x and 5x figures are abstract-only, model-specific (Qwen3.5/Nemotron-3) and workload-specific; no absolute latencies are given, and an abstract is not a benchmark.

### 2611.23678 — Evaluating Governed LLM-Driven ML Experimentation
**Claim:** Agents running ML experiments are usually judged only by their best score, which hides whether failed trials were kept or decisions traced. The paper builds a governed harness in which a control plane, not the proposing LLM, owns each trial's lifecycle, checks edit scope, decides keep or discard, and appends one record per trial to an append-only ledger. Governance metrics (invalid-action rate, repeated-bad rate, provenance completeness) are computed from that ledger; across six ML tasks and 1,445 trials the same contract applied unchanged.
**Native relevance:** P3 (append-only replication journal) and P6 (eval harness) — the closest architectural match in the batch. `history.db`'s `shard_events` (55 events, no node identity, sequence number, or sync state) and the unwired `journal_mirror.py` are the ungoverned precursor the paper warns about. One ledger record per write event, with scope, keep/discard, and provenance completeness, is the design P3 needs.
**Verdict:** ADOPT — the mechanism fits Native's constraints as-is. An append-only SQLite ledger owned by `core.py` (the control plane), not by the calling agent, needs no paid service, no external API, and no new storage engine.
**Does NOT prove:** An abstract is not a benchmark. The 1,445-trial figures and the 43-of-50 trials lost by success-only logging cannot be verified from the abstract alone — no per-task breakdown or ledger schema is given.

### 2611.18627 — FlashREINFORCE
**Claim:** FlashREINFORCE is a critic-free single-rollout asynchronous reinforcement learning framework for agentic language models, combining One-Batch REINFORCE, a Sequence Trust Region, and Sample-Mean Optimization. It stays stable through 6,000 updates on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B at policy lag four and 1,450 on the 30B MoE Qwen3-30B-A3B at lag eight; under identical loss, data, and lag it matches a strengthened group-relative baseline within 0.5 points with half the rollouts, scores 38.0 versus 36.3 five-benchmark mean on Qwen2.5-Math-1.5B, and reaches 98.3%/96.5% seen/unseen ALFWorld success on Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct.
**Native relevance:** None directly. Native is a storage and retrieval engine, not a training framework: there is no policy, no rollout, no reward, and no gradient update anywhere in `core.py`. The closest adjacency is that Native stores the trajectories an agent would learn from — but the paper says nothing about memory, retrieval, or how experience is stored, and Native has no training consumer for those trajectories today.
**Verdict:** REJECT — a training method for agentic language models is out of scope for a SQLite memory engine; adopting it would mean building a training loop the directive never asks for. The only honest carry-over is the observation that async, stale, variable-length trajectories can still train stably, relevant only if a future lane ever trains on Native's stored experience, which is not a P1–P6 item.
**Does NOT prove:** All numbers (0.5 points, 38.0 vs 36.3, 98.3%/96.5% ALFWorld) are abstract-reported and unverifiable from the abstract alone — no variance, no list of the five benchmarks, no definition of the "strengthened baseline." Nothing in the abstract connects single-rollout RL to retrieval quality or memory systems.
